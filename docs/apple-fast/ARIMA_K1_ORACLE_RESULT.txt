Source-level model issue before blaming inverses
-----------------------------------------------
The full K1 harness receives the same rounded Q32/P0/alpha0 arrays as the
serial reference, but receiving the same inputs is not enough to make
the recurrences the same mathematical model:

1. Q32 is constructed as (R*sigma)[:,None]*R[None,:], with rounding between
multiplications. In real arithmetic it is rank-one symmetric PSD; lifted
Q32 need not be exactly symmetric, rank one or positive semidefinite.
2. K1 leaves follow the Metal source's pz*pz^T convention. For nonsymmetric
Q this is not even Q - Q[:,0]*Q[0,:]/Q00. The formula assumes symmetry.
3. The serial reference, deliberately mirroring production layout,
symmetrizes every predictive P and replaces every diagonal by abs(diag).
The scan performs neither per-time repair. A general Gaussian monoid
presumes a valid covariance model; it does not represent arbitrary
nonlinear covariance repair at each original time step.

These are concrete source differences. The four sigma-only math failures
are consistent with changed Q rounding/symmetry. The near-pair failure
and repeated-MA sensitivity also make conditioning/indefinite covariance
plausible, but saved reports do not contain Q asymmetry, eigenvalues,
repair counts or solve condition/residual diagnostics. Do NOT claim that
conditioning, abs repairs or asymmetry has been experimentally isolated.
A malformed covariance model can amplify both scan and serial numerical
error. Neither diagonal jitter nor relaxed thresholds resolves model match.
