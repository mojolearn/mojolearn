`FUSED_REFUSED_REGIME` anywhere**, so the regime bound is retired by
measurement. Replicated on a second pod at a different commit: 3,697 again.

**The refusals are FALSE POSITIVES.** Removing the backward corner refusal
gives BIT-IDENTICAL loss, gradients, parameters, m, v and flags across all 700

(2.298x, tail now equals head); `eager_bytes` stays 432 with zero layers grown;

to **1,349 GPU-hours**, about $3,500 to $4,700.

`bwd_corner_refuses=True, kv_guard=False, sticky=False`. Main's kernels are
byte-for-byte unchanged. The speedup is proven to EXIST; it is NOT proven safe
to ship, and deleting a safety check on one shape's 700 steps is not a licence
to stop checking. **The shippable change is a CORRECT CORNER PREDICATE.** Next
measurement, already named: give each of the three backward kernels its own
