Mojolearn native array-layout checks, 2026-11-10

45 independent fixtures, direct core.native_array_layout APIs
  18 literal layouts: rank-zero scalar, vector, C/F matrices, singleton axes,
  zero axes in each position, ordinary empty strides, representable zero
  strides with a MAX_INT axis, MAX_INT byte count with one-byte items, and
  native_matrix_shape vector/empty/one-column conventions.
  Check count, nbytes, itemsize, order, every shape/stride and deep metadata
  copies. Huge shapes allocate only metadata Lists, never backing data.
  9 supported dtype widths and buffer codes: f8/i8/f4/i4/u4/f2/u2/u1/i1.
  6 unsupported dtype spellings/widths/endian formats refuse through both
  native_array_itemsize and native_array_code.
  12 named refusals: negative axes including after zero, element-count and
  byte-count overflow, zero/negative item width, invalid order, C/F stride
  multiplication overflow on zero-count shapes, including near-limit cases.
  In particular [0, MAX_INT] C and [MAX_INT, 0] F with itemsize 2 must refuse.
  Safe reversed-order counterparts with zero strides remain admitted.
