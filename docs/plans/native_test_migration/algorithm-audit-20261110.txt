     final host coefficient vector into caller-owned output. The prior
     bindings/classical_tsqr.mojo at a209b57325^:438–440 returned its owned

     caller-buffer contract; TSQR routing limits cols to 511. Producer informed
     to assess final CPU elementwise output directly into caller storage.
     This is a bounded source cost, not a measured regression or a reason to
     weaken ownership. No product edits made by this audit lane.
  3. checks/native_tests/archive_minmax.mojo:93: expected NaN literal
     0x7FF8000000000000042 exceeds UInt64. Archive owner confirmed the typo
     and is fixing it to the intended 0x7FF8000000000042. This is a fixture
