## Noted, not changed (native code, needs a compile)

- `StepLR.lr_at(0)` / `ExponentialLR.lr_at(0)` raise a bare `Exception("schedule step t is ONE-BASED")` although
  the docstring promises `ValueError`: `bindings/lr_table_helpers.mojo:192` builds the `ValueError:` error, but the
  re-raise through `py_call` at `bindings/optimizer_schedule_native.mojo:113` drops the type.
- `StepLR(0.1, step_size="2")` parses the str; `StepLR(-0.1, ...)` and `ExponentialLR(0.1, gamma=float("nan"))`
  construct and refuse only at `lr_at` (`OneCycleLR` refuses at construction).
- `Adafactor(params, lr="1")` raises a bare `Exception: must be real number, not str` (the class of defect fixed for
  `ops.Session` on Oct 21).
- `GaussianNB(var_smoothing=-1.0).fit` is accepted (scikit-learn refuses it); `IterativeImputer(max_iter=-1)` and
  `IterativeImputer().fit` with `inf` are accepted; `MaxAbsScaler().fit_transform` accepts `inf` (scikit-learn refuses).
