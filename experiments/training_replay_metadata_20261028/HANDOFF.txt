Repeated work removed:
- replay_values previously cleared and recopied each node's integer and float
  argument slices on every replay/minibatch. PreparedProgramReplay captures
  those native vectors once per admitted fit and passes them by borrow.
- Dynamic handle/value containers retain capacity across minibatches instead
  of allocating new scratch per replay. Handles still resolve from the current
  inputs, parameters and earlier node results in original node order.
- The repeated fallback dropout-stride selection is computed once per replay.

Setup, reuse and invalidation:
- train_program_control builds one body inventory and, when conditional, one
  inventory each for predicate and otherwise graph after its existing public
  validation, before acquired training buffers. They belong only to this fit's
  immutable graph snapshots; no cross-call graph cache or mutation generation
  is introduced by the training integration.
- The legacy replay_values interface remains a one-shot wrapper that prepares
  its own inventory. Other callers keep their behavior/interface; they do not
  gain cross-call preparation reuse in this batch.
- The standalone helper may also be owned by immutable inference program
  lifecycle caches (root's separate ops_api batch), invalidated on drop/close
  or replacement. This branch does not edit ops_api.mojo or program.mojo.
- Predicate and branch selection, optimizer/epoch ordering and loss/backward
  paths are unchanged. Preparing metadata does not execute unselected branches
  or eagerly validate operation-specific arguments. Captured graph structural
  offsets remain the existing admission contract.
- Dropout's final integer is recomputed from original_last_int each replay:
  original + (step * stride + random_offset + node). No prior minibatch offset
  accumulates. Negative-step predicate/inference calls restore the original
  key. Existing conditional stride/offset and persistent epoch/minibatch step
  expressions are unchanged. Empty dropout argument vectors still fail at
  the selected replay position, not during preparation.

Ownership/errors:
- Each inventory owns its integer/float metadata and scratch Lists. No raw
  source pointer, Python object or callback is introduced. Dynamic handle words
  are non-owning: existing temps/tape cleanup still owns their retirement.
- The inventories remain alive throughout synchronous node dispatch. Separate
  predicate/body/otherwise scratch cannot contaminate another component.
  An exception exits through the existing training cleanup; native metadata
  Lists are released by ordinary ownership. Concurrent replays must not share
