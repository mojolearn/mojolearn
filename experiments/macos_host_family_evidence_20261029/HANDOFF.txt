job or mere SDK equality. Original source/binary evidence is still reconciled
and retained, but missing toolchain provenance remains unqualified. No assumption
that worker counts or O0/default flags are equivalent is made.

builder. build_host_family.sh invokes Mojo/LLVM with apple-m1, macOS minimum11.0

explicit and matches the existing version-level Apple toolchain record.

unresolved dependency and checks the captured lock against the frozen source.
