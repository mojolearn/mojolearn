PCS native numerical descriptor, 2026-10-28

Appends operation pcs_draw at ID82, preserving IDs0..81 including mode77 and
capabilities81. Schema: two addresses (index/sign outputs), three integers
(seed,n_components,degree*n_features), no floats. Capability bit3 captures
XN_IDN_SKETCH_CTR; old binaries without this bit refuse the missing operation
with the existing public rebuild message before any hash-output allocation.

The host auxiliary owner repeats the retained wrapper's admission and calls
op_kfeat_pcs_draw_idn_host. The device auxiliary owner calls kfeat_pcs_draw_idn,
which retains its own dimension checks, allocations, kernel, downloads and
synchronization. Existing arithmetic, compile-time switches and device choice
are unchanged. The host's historical components*total bound and the device's
total-only bound remain distinct. Both reject null output addresses.

The operation owner captures the old compiled entry only on the unchanged
legacy module route. With an admitted native provider, it reads captured
capability metadata and dispatches pcs_draw through the typed native table;
index/sign array owners remain live through dispatch and fitted publication.
The default/legacy route still makes a CPython numerical extension call until
the shared mode/admission repair promotes normal native routing. This commit
does not change that setting or claim a complete PCS operation migration.

Generated schema and both tables are produced by tools/gen_x_neighbors_provider.py.
Source-only generation/ID/schema/call-closure checks are evidence of source
consistency, not compilation, numerical correctness or vendor identity.
