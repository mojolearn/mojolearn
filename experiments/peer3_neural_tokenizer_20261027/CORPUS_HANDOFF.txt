Behavior and retained routes
The existing tokenizer train/encode/document-cut/gather native algorithms are
called directly. Cache keys, ordered manifest fields, canonical rank identity,
atomic sibling publication and readonly mmap ownership are retained. Public
prefetch remains lazy; native field errors occur at the requested step and close
that iterator. Checkpoint matching reads only schedule/vocabulary metadata, not
unrelated model weights. Scalar/path/buffer hooks, replaced __dict__ descriptors,
progress callbacks and overridden prefetch ids methods are refused before calls.
Python runtime progress callbacks are intentionally unsupported; returned manifest
counters/timings are the native alternative.
