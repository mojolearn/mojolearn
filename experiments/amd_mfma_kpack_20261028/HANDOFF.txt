## Findings that change the brief's premise
- The report's diagnosis 3 (2c "AMD fill") is out of date. Roadmap A1 already shipped on main as the default-on MFMA16 small-tile body (`identical_gemm_mfma16_kernel`, lane/nr-gemm). A2's fill selection also shipped: `gemm_mfma16_waves`, plus NVIDIA's `IDN_GEMM_NV_STEP_KPACK`. AMD stepped-down calls already run on the matrix cores. Both shipped arms are marked "NEVER RUN — PENDING MEASUREMENT".
- K>1 fp32 MFMA (`32x32x2f32`, `16x16x4f32`) cannot keep the bits. Its internal sum order and rounding are not specified by the ISA and have never been probed. The contract needs one `fma_rn` per p in ascending order. A fold defined by undocumented hardware cannot be reproduced on NVIDIA, Apple or the host, so this is not done as a bits-change idea either (REPORT section 3, "reject"). On gfx942 the K=1 multi-block forms issue products at the same rate as the K>1 forms, so K=1 costs no matrix throughput. "kpack" here therefore means more cells per lane. Per p, a lane reads 1 A word and 1 B word for 2048 products of its wave, against 2 words for 1024 with MFMA16: half the shared-memory reads per product.
- Expect a small or null effect on the board shapes. At 2048x384x384 the device is under-filled and the rule declines (384 slabs < 1024). The rule takes 2048x1536x384 and the split weight-gradient calls when the workspace fits. gemm 4096^3 runs the existing 128x128 matrix-core kernel and is not affected. The report's "43 -> 20-28 ms" was based on the out-of-date premise.

## Bits
Arm B (define ON) should give the same bits as arm A (define OFF) by construction (the comment at the section head). Each cell has one accumulator, seeded +0.0 per leaf, with one K=1 MFMA `fma_rn` step per p in ascending order. Each step is flushed (or admitted), the leaf partial ftz goes through `_fold_push_local`, and ftz(root) is stored. Split mode is the MFMA16 leaf-major ws plus `_ksplit_fold_launch`. Only the tile and the cells per lane change. This is not verified on a device.
