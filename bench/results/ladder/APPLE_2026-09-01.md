# The classical lanes at scale on Apple: two answers, both negative

The gap this closes: every classical lane had only ever been timed at its own
correctness fixture, a few dozen rows, where the number is per-call fixed cost
and we lose 13x to 35x. Nobody had run them at a size where a GPU could
plausibly win, so nobody knew.

Ladder declared in the source before any run, every rung run, every rung
printed. `nice -n 19`, one lane at a time, arms alternated inside each rung.

## rbfsampler: LOSES AT EVERY RUNG, AND THE RATIO IS FLAT

| n | ours | sklearn CPU | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | 2.11 ms | 0.32 ms | 0.15x |
| 10,000 | 4.92 ms | 1.46 ms | 0.30x |
| 100,000 | 41.09 ms | 11.76 ms | 0.29x |
| 1,000,000 | 427.35 ms | 126.06 ms | 0.30x |

Bands do not overlap at any rung.

**The flatness is the finding.** From 10,000 to 1,000,000 the ratio sits at
0.29 to 0.30 and does not move. A launch-overhead story improves with scale
because the fixed cost amortizes. This does not, so it is not fixed cost. It
is a throughput loss of about 3.4x, and scale will not rescue it.

At the correctness fixture this lane read 19.04x slower, which invited the
reading that the number was all overhead. It was not; most of it was, and a
real 3.4x sits underneath.

## nystroem: CANNOT RUN AT SCALE AT ALL

Ladder stopped after the first rung:

    km_basis_indices: n_samples 10000 exceeds KM_MAX_BASIS_POOL = 4096

That is a hard capability limit, not a speed result. The lane refuses any
fixture above 4,096 rows, so there is no workload at which it could win and
the question of its throughput does not arise until the cap is lifted.

At 1,000 rows it is 0.081x, 21.6 ms against 1.75 ms.

**This is the more useful of the two results.** The recorded 13.87x at the
correctness fixture, and the 6.71x win over cuML on an H100, are both
measured at shapes far below the cap. Neither says anything about a real
workload, because a real workload is refused.

## What this does and does not settle

SETTLED: rbfsampler does not win on Apple at any size up to a million rows,
and its loss is throughput rather than overhead. nystroem cannot be asked the
question until KM_MAX_BASIS_POOL moves.

NOT SETTLED: gmm, kde, spectral and gp. Those ladders are built and unrun.

NOT MEASURED HERE AT ALL: whether either lane wins on a discrete GPU. Both of
these are Apple numbers against a CPU, and the H100 rows in
`bench/results/fast_speed/` are a different comparison at a different shape.
