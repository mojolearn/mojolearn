GBDT Metal fit times, Apple M4, 2026-09-15 19:39 to 19:42 EDT

THIS IS A FIRST BASELINE, NOT A PROVEN HEALTHY FIGURE.

It exists because the repo had no GBDT Metal timing at all, so when fits went
about 20x slow on Sep 15 there was nothing to measure the slowdown against.
These numbers were taken once the machine was known good again, meaning the
degraded state had cleared, so a future slowdown has a reference point. They
are not a target, not a claim about how fast this path should be, and the
figure "about 1 s per fit" that circulated on Sep 15 is unverified and must not
be quoted. See docs/lanes/LANE_STATUS_lane-metal-queue-leak.md.

Conditions
  mojolearn    identical tier, vendor metal, the shared checkout's built
               bindings (python/mojolearn/identical/_mojolearn_gbdt.so)
  isolation    alone under the Metal lock (scratchpad mac_slot.sh metal), one
               core, nice -n 19, no other GPU job
  machine      Apple M4, macOS 26.5.2, uptime 9 days at the time of the run

Small fixture: 2000 rows x 8 features, n_estimators=20, max_depth=6, Logloss,
default grow policy. Five fits in one process, milliseconds:

  7185.8  7066.9  6746.9  6999.7  7159.8

Large fixture: 20000 rows x 16 features, n_estimators=20, max_depth=6, Logloss.
Three fits per grow policy in one process, seconds, beside the degraded numbers
from the same script and shape earlier that afternoon
(~/mojolearn-evidence/gbdt-metal-slowdown-2026-09-15/):

  policy          this baseline            degraded Sep 15 afternoon
  SymmetricTree   8.289  7.735  8.288      21.5 to 23.4
  Depthwise       11.848 14.082 13.373     27.0 to 30.4

So this baseline is about 2.7x faster than the degraded state on SymmetricTree
and about 2.2x on Depthwise.

Metal command queues, `ioclasscount AGXCommandQueue`, sampled around both runs:
34 before each run, 34 after every individual fit, 34 the moment each process
exited, and 34 again 20 to 30 s later. Eleven fits across two processes added
none, which is why the per-call DeviceContext in the GBDT binding path is not
the source of the queue pileup investigated in that lane.
