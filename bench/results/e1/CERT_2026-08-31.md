Apple M4 (`2026-08-31_180957`), AMD MI325X (`2026-08-31_221142`), NVIDIA
(`nv_partial_2026-08-31`, RunPod, host driver 580).

refactor.

## Why NVIDIA stopped: a hang, and it is ours

`check_hw_zero_series_keeps_start`, the sixth gate of
`holtwinters/checks/hw_check.mojo` under IDENTICAL, **deadlocks on NVIDIA**.
It is not slow and it is not compiling:

    ps    39:43 elapsed, 00:01:24 CPU, 3.5%, state S
    wchan futex_wait_queue
    nvidia-smi  0 % utilization, 398 MiB
    threads 99

CPU time was 00:01:24 at two samples four minutes apart -- frozen, not
advancing. The GPU is idle. The process is asleep on a futex. The five gates
before it all pass on that box and print normally, the last being
`check_hw_signed_zero_clamp`; `arima` had already completed its whole card
on the same box in 23 seconds.

It consumed 40 minutes of a one-hour lease and the box was terminated rather
than paid to keep waiting. That is what cost holtwinters, spectral and tsa
their NVIDIA column, and rerunning the leg unchanged will lose another lease
the same way.

**This is an OPEN DEFECT, not an infra failure**, and it must not be written
off as one under `[[runpod-failure-is-not-invalidation]]`: no balance, no
driver, no provisioning and no timeout is involved. The gate hangs on one
vendor. Whether the hang is in the gate or in the thing it gates is not yet
known, and until it is, nothing about holtwinters on NVIDIA is claimed in
either direction.

Next step is a short leg that runs ONLY `check_hw_zero_series_keeps_start`
on NVIDIA under IDENTICAL with a timeout and a stack dump, so the lease is
spent on the diagnosis rather than on the queue behind it.
