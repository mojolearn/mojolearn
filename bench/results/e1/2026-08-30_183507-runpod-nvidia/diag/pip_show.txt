Name: mojolearn
Version: 0.3.0
Summary: GPU machine learning in Mojo. Gradient boosting, random forests, extra trees, SVM, k-means, k-NN, DBSCAN, PCA, hierarchical and spectral clustering, GLMs, scoring metrics and an FP32 matmul, on Apple silicon through Metal and from the same source on NVIDIA and AMD. Three numeric modes per estimator: fast, deterministic (same bits every run) and identical (same bits on every GPU vendor)
