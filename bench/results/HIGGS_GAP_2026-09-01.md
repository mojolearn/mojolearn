# Where the higgs AUC gap comes from: it compounds with BOOSTING ITERATIONS

Both arms matched throughout (`bootstrap_type='No'`, `random_strength=0`,
`boosting_type='Plain'` on CatBoost's side, which is what ours already runs),
full 8,800,000 x 28 train split, 2,200,000 test, one process, M4.

| trees | depth | ours | CatBoost CPU | delta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 6 | 0.80027628 | 0.80093602 | -0.00066 |
| 100 | 8 | 0.80786443 | 0.80884582 | -0.00098 |
| 500 | 6 | 0.81722528 | 0.82210075 | -0.00488 |
| 500 | 8 | 0.82160984 | 0.83046108 | -0.00885 |

## The two effects, separated

    depth 6 -> 8   at 100 trees:  -0.00066 -> -0.00098   x1.48
    depth 6 -> 8   at 500 trees:  -0.00488 -> -0.00885   x1.81
    100 -> 500 trees at depth 6:  -0.00066 -> -0.00488   x7.4
    100 -> 500 trees at depth 8:  -0.00098 -> -0.00885   x9.0

**PORTING.md item 140's prediction is CONFIRMED, and it is not the whole
story.** The gap does grow with depth, at both tree counts, which is what
that entry predicts: its blast radius is "leaves still moving at step six,
extreme leaves, which are exactly the ones a deep tree makes many of".

But BOOSTING ITERATIONS dominate, by roughly 5x. Going from 100 to 500 trees
multiplies the gap by 7.4 to 9.0; going from depth 6 to depth 8 multiplies it
by only 1.48 to 1.81.

The two effects are very nearly SEPARABLE, which is the useful part:

    0.00066 x 7.4  = 0.00488   (observed 0.00488)
    0.00488 x 1.81 = 0.00885   (observed 0.00885)
    0.00066 x 1.48 = 0.00098   (observed 0.00098)
    0.00098 x 9.0  = 0.00885   (observed 0.00885)

A gap that multiplies cleanly in each factor independently is the signature
of a SMALL PER-TREE ERROR THAT ACCUMULATES DOWN THE BOOSTING SEQUENCE, with
depth setting how large each tree's contribution is. It is not a threshold, a
single bad split, or a shape-specific artifact -- any of those would show up
as a jump rather than as a clean product.

## What this rules in and out

RULED OUT, on 2026-08-31: configuration. Matching CatBoost's regularization
moved CatBoost UP and widened the gap
(`bench/results/higgs_matched_config_2026-08-31.json`). `boosting_type`
resolved to Plain by itself.

RULED IN: a per-iteration leaf-value divergence. Item 140 is the one named
mechanism of that shape, and the one already measured moving this number: the
Float32 acceptance-width fix moved AUC +0.00037. Their CPU Newton walk
freezes at six accepted steps against a `FastLogf`-valued metric; ours is
their GPU's non-strict test and stalls later.

NOT ESTABLISHED: that item 140 is the whole gap. The +0.00037 it is known to
be worth is 4% of the 500x8 gap. What this sweep establishes is the SHAPE of
the residual, and the shape is consistent with it.

## The next experiment, and it needs no rented box

Set `leaf_estimation_iterations=6` on our arm, which is where CatBoost CPU
actually freezes, and re-run the four cells. If item 140 is the driver, the
gap should collapse most at 500x8 and barely move at 100x6. If the gap is
flat under that change, item 140 is not the driver and the next suspect is
DEVIATION 135's border subsample, which draws a different 200,000 rows than
theirs at the same seed.
