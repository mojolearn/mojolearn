LIMITS
A historical audit supports the old pairing. It does not automatically approve reuse against a new, unmeasured own arm. Current effective parameters, data identity/actual shape, clock, retained source/output evidence and machine/resource records still matter. Missing metadata stays unresolved; library dates or slow hosts alone do not force reruns.
A review failure may require a new measurement; a current broken pairing may be repairable using its retained opponent. Therefore 100 is the currently identified gap, not a final rerun schedule.
10 fit and 20 inference LightGBM CPU tree cells are explicitly refused by the driver and excluded from the runnable inventory. Algorithm-family DART LightGBM arms are retained.
Inventory scans 3471 local board/store/archive files. No CPU or GPU opponent benchmarks were launched; no remote machines were changed.

SMOKE
Preflight constructs/probes arms and compares effective parameter settings, required library/version readbacks, implementation and GPU hardware evidence. It does not time CPU fits. STARTED is incomplete, not passed.
Default gpu_gap_campaign smoke selects GPU-opponent recipes. CPU fallback recipes require cpu_fallback selection; Apple identical and fast are separate mode workers.
Reduced execution smoke additionally checks completion, params MATCHED, expected cells, times and quality availability, with named unsupported/refused exceptions. It does not certify a full-dataset speedup or blanket historical timing reuse.
The opponent store compares settings/data keys plus fresh readback/version/device before reuse. Default bench_board skips missing opponents; the campaign timing wrapper adds with-opponents when given a store or retime-opponents without one, so that wrapper is not reuse-only.
