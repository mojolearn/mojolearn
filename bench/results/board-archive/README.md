# Board archive

Every bench board as it stood before the board was replaced by a run on a newer wheel. The live board shows only
the latest wheel; older boards move here and are never deleted or edited.

Layout: `<box>/<root>/` with `board/BOARD.md` (the table), `board/board.json.gz` (every cell), and the root's
`resume-config.json.gz` (wheel version, plan), `coverage.json.gz` and `exceptions.json.gz` where they existed.
`2026-09-29_*` are the full single-run boards of Sep 29; `board-resume*` are the resumable roots (0.8.25 on NVIDIA
and AMD, 0.8.31 then 0.8.32 on the Macs and NVIDIA).

Opponent measurements are also kept separately, deduplicated, in `../opponent-archive/`.
