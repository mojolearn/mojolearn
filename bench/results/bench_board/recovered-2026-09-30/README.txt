Recovered external board measurements, 2026-09-30

NVIDIA L40S, AMD MI325X, Apple M2 Pro and M3 Ultra. Includes full runs, targeted supplements and explicitly named proof/smoke runs. Do not combine small proof runs with full-data measurements. Original board records, raw race JSON, opponent stores and logs are preserved. MANIFEST.json records file sizes and SHA-256 digests.
